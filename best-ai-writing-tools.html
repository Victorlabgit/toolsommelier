The best AI writing tools for marketing, paired to your team

Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic and Grammarly compared: which AI writing tool fits marketing teams, SEO content, sales workflows or everyday writing.

General chat assistants like ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini can write almost anything. So why pay for a dedicated AI writing tool? Because specialized tools add what marketing work needs: brand voice, templates, SEO features, team workflows.

Here's which one pairs with which kind of team.

The short answer

  • Marketing teams that need a consistent brand voice: Jasper.
  • Sales and go-to-market workflows: Copy.ai.
  • SEO blog content: Writesonic.
  • Polishing everyday writing (emails, docs): Grammarly.

Best for marketing teams

Jasper

Best for brand-consistent content across a marketing team. Free option: free trial.

Refined, with a signature house style.

Jasper is built for marketing teams: you teach it your brand voice, style guide and product knowledge, and every piece of content follows it. Campaign tools and collaboration make it a fit for teams producing content at volume.

Why we like it
  • Brand voice and style guide built in
  • Campaign and team features
  • Many marketing templates
Watch out for
  • Priced for teams more than solo users
  • Overkill if you only write occasionally
Visit Jasper

Best for sales workflows

Copy.ai

Best for automating go-to-market and sales content. Free option: yes, limited.

Energetic and efficient, built for volume.

Copy.ai has moved beyond copywriting toward go-to-market workflows: automating prospect research, outreach and sales content at scale. It suits sales and growth teams that want repeatable processes rather than one-off texts.

Why we like it
  • Workflow automation for sales and marketing
  • Free plan to test
  • Good for repeatable, high-volume tasks
Watch out for
  • Workflows take time to set up
  • Less focused on long-form editorial writing
Visit Copy.ai

Well-structured, made to rank.

Writesonic focuses on SEO content: researching topics, generating structured articles and optimizing them for search. It's a good fit for bloggers and content marketers whose main goal is organic traffic.

Best for everyday writing

Grammarly

Best for emails, documents and messages, everywhere you type. Free option: yes.

A smooth everyday companion.

Grammarly works where you already write (browser, email, documents) to fix grammar, clarity and tone, and adds generative AI to draft and rewrite. It's the pick for improving everyday writing rather than producing marketing content.

Why we like it
  • Works in your browser, email and docs
  • Excellent grammar and tone suggestions
  • Strong free version
Watch out for
  • Not a full content-marketing platform
  • Advanced suggestions on paid plans
Visit Grammarly

How to choose

  • Several people writing for one brand? Jasper.
  • Sales outreach at scale? Copy.ai.
  • Blog traffic is the goal? Writesonic.
  • You just want to write better emails and docs? Grammarly, or a general assistant.
  • Whatever you pick, fact-check. AI writing tools can state wrong facts confidently.

FAQ

Is a dedicated AI writing tool better than ChatGPT?

For occasional writing, a general assistant is often enough. Dedicated tools pay off when you need brand voice, templates, SEO features or team workflows.

What is the best AI writing tool for SEO?

Writesonic is built around SEO content, with research and optimization features aimed at ranking articles.

Is there a free AI writing tool?

Grammarly and Copy.ai offer free plans; Jasper offers a free trial.
