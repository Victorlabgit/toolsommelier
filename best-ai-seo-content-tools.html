Looking for the best AI SEO tools for content writers? A general chatbot can draft an article, but it can't tell you which subtopics the pages already ranking on Google cover, which terms you're missing, or whether ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews ever mention your brand. That's the job of AI content optimization tools: they analyse the search results for your keyword, turn them into a brief and score your draft as you write.

We compared four of them using their official documentation and plans, without inventing test scores. If you mainly need a writing assistant rather than an SEO workflow, start with our guide to the best AI writing tools for marketing instead.

The short answer

  • Best all-round content optimizer, with AI search tracking: Surfer.
  • Best for research, briefs and topic clusters: Frase.
  • Best for solo bloggers and small sites: NeuronWriter.
  • Best if you also repurpose content for social and email: Semrush Content Toolkit.

Best overall

Surfer

Best for writers and teams who want a real-time content score plus AI search tracking. Free option: free tools (AI detector, Chrome extension); check the pricing page for plan trials.

Full-bodied and precise, the reference pour of the category.

Surfer (now at surfer.ai) is built around its Content Editor: pick a keyword and it scores your draft against the pages that rank, suggesting terms, headings and length as you type. Around it, Surfer has added keyword research, a topical map, a content audit for existing pages, the Surfy AI writer and an AI Tracker that follows how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. Plans differ in how many pages and AI prompts you can track, so match the tier to your site's size.

Why we like it
  • Mature, real-time Content Editor
  • AI Tracker for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews
  • Content audit to refresh existing articles
Watch out for
  • Tracking limits depend on the plan
  • Easy to over-optimize if you chase the score instead of the reader

Best for research and briefs

Frase

Best for content strategists who plan topic clusters and write detailed briefs. Free option: 7-day free trial, no card required (per Frase).

Bright and structured, with a long research finish.

Frase started as a research-and-brief tool and has grown into a broader platform: SEO research, topic clusters, content optimization for both search engines and AI answers (what Frase calls SEO and GEO), AI visibility tracking, a site audit and an AI agent. It integrates with WordPress, Webflow, Sanity and Wix. Plans are sized by articles, audit pages and seats, which makes the cost easy to forecast for a content calendar.

Why we like it
  • Strong research and brief-building workflow
  • Optimizes for both Google and AI answers
  • Clear plan limits by articles and seats
Watch out for
  • No permanent free plan for the core product
  • The entry plan has a low monthly article allowance
Visit Frase

Best for solo bloggers

NeuronWriter

Best for bloggers and small sites who want optimization without an enterprise suite. Free option: 7-day free trial (per NeuronWriter).

Approachable and honest, a well-made everyday bottle.

NeuronWriter focuses on the core job: its Content Designer analyses the search results and gives you terms, questions and structure to cover, and its Content Writer and AI assistant help you draft inside the same editor. Content Ideas suggests topics based on search intent, and higher plans add Google Search Console, WordPress and Shopify integrations plus AI visibility monitoring. Plans are organised by projects and monthly analyses, which suits one person running a few sites.

Why we like it
  • Focused editor that's quick to learn
  • Plans built around projects and analyses
  • Integrations with Search Console, WordPress and Shopify on higher plans
Watch out for
  • Entry plan leaves out content ideas and Search Console integration
  • Fewer site-level tools (no site audit listed)
Visit NeuronWriter

Best for repurposing

Semrush

Best for marketers who write, optimize and repurpose content from one place. Free option: 7-day free trial (per Semrush).

A generous blend that pairs with the whole menu.

Semrush's Content Toolkit is sold as its own toolkit, separate from the classic Semrush SEO toolkit. It covers the whole content loop: a Topic Finder backed by search data, an SEO Brief Generator, an AI article generator, a Content Optimizer for SEO and AI search, saved brand voices, and repurposing into social posts, newsletters and Google Business Profile posts, with export to Google Docs and WordPress. It suits marketers who want one tool from idea to distribution rather than a pure optimization editor.

Why we like it
  • Idea-to-publication workflow in one toolkit
  • Brand voices and repurposing for social and email
  • Backed by Semrush search data
Watch out for
  • Deeper SEO research (backlinks, rank tracking) sits in other Semrush toolkits
  • A monthly cap applies to SEO-boosted articles

How to choose

  • You want the most established optimization editor and to see how AI assistants mention you? Surfer.
  • Your bottleneck is research and briefs for several writers? Frase.
  • You run one or a few blogs on your own? NeuronWriter.
  • You also turn each article into social posts and newsletters? Semrush Content Toolkit.
  • Don't write for the score. These tools tell you what ranking pages cover; your own expertise, examples and accuracy are what make a page worth ranking. Pair any of them with a writing assistant from our AI writing tools guide if you need help drafting.

FAQ

What is the best AI SEO tool for content writers?

For most writers, Surfer is the safest all-round pick thanks to its Content Editor and AI search tracking. Frase is better if research and briefs are your main job, and NeuronWriter suits solo bloggers.

Do AI SEO tools help you rank in ChatGPT and AI Overviews?

They can help you cover a topic thoroughly and track whether AI assistants mention you: Surfer, Frase and NeuronWriter all offer AI visibility monitoring. None can guarantee a citation.

Is there a free AI SEO content tool?

None of the four offers a full free plan for its core optimizer. Frase, NeuronWriter and Semrush offer 7-day free trials, and Surfer offers free tools such as an AI detector and a Chrome extension.

Is Surfer or Frase better?

Surfer is stronger as a real-time optimization editor with AI search tracking; Frase leans toward research, briefs and topic clusters. Many teams choose based on whether writing or planning is their bottleneck.

Can I just use ChatGPT for SEO content?

ChatGPT can draft and outline, but it doesn't analyse the current search results for your keyword or score your draft against them. SEO content tools add that layer.
