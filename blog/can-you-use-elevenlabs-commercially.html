Can you use ElevenLabs commercially? What the free and paid plans allow

Can you use ElevenLabs commercially? Only on a paid plan. Here is what the free plan allows, the attribution rule, and the catches to know before you publish.

ElMaSp
Voice, November 27, 2026, 5 min read

Contains affiliate links. Disclosure

The short answer

Yes, but only with a paid plan. ElevenLabs says all paid plans include a commercial license (Beta features excepted), while the free plan cannot be used for any commercial purpose and requires you to credit ElevenLabs when you share what you make.

ElevenLabs makes some of the most natural AI voices available, so it is no surprise that YouTubers, podcasters, course creators and small businesses want to use them in work that earns money. Before you publish, the question to settle is simple: can you use ElevenLabs commercially, and on which plan? Here is what ElevenLabs' own help center says, in plain English.

The short rule: paid plans yes, free plan no

ElevenLabs' help article on publishing content is unusually direct. The free plan "does not include a commercial license and cannot be used for any commercial purpose." On the other side, "all paid plans include a commercial license, provided you're not using Beta Services." In other words, even the entry-level paid tier unlocks commercial use; you do not need a business or enterprise plan just to monetize a voiceover.

"Commercial" should be read broadly. A monetized YouTube channel, a paid course, an ad, a client project, a product demo on your company website or an audiobook you sell all count. If money is involved, directly or indirectly, assume it is commercial and work from a paid plan.

What you can do on the free plan

The free plan is still useful for testing voices, personal projects and non-commercial sharing. The condition is attribution: if you publish content made on the free plan (or while signed out), ElevenLabs asks you to credit it by including "elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai" in the title. Music generated with Eleven Music has its own variant: you reference "Eleven Music" when you distribute it.

So a free-plan narration for a school project or a hobby video you share for fun is fine, as long as the credit is there. A free-plan voiceover in a sponsored video is not.

Three catches people miss

  • Upgrading later does not cover old audio. The help center states that content "created outside of a paid subscription (before or after)" cannot be used commercially. If you generated a voiceover on the free plan, regenerate it while subscribed before using it in paid work.
  • Content made while subscribed stays licensed. The flip side is good news: ElevenLabs says content generated during a paid subscription can be used commercially "indefinitely," subject to its Service-Specific Terms. Cancelling later does not take that right away.
  • Beta features are excluded. Output from features labelled as Beta Services cannot be used commercially or in production, even on a paid plan. Check whether the model or feature you use carries a Beta label.

The license is not the only rule

A commercial license covers ElevenLabs' side of things; it does not give you rights you don't already have. ElevenLabs makes commercial use conditional on you holding the necessary rights to what you put in and following its Terms of Service and Prohibited Use Policy. In practice: only clone your own voice or a voice you have clear permission to use, make sure you own or license the script you are reading, and do not use voices to impersonate real people. For anything high-stakes, such as a broadcast ad or a large client contract, read the current terms on the official ElevenLabs website or ask a lawyer.

Which plan should you pick?

Since every paid plan includes the commercial license, the real choice is about volume and features: how many characters of audio you generate each month, whether you need professional-grade voice cloning, and how many people share the account. Plans and credit allowances change often, so compare them on the official ElevenLabs pricing page rather than relying on figures quoted in older articles. A sensible approach is to test voices on the free plan, then subscribe for the month you produce your commercial project.

Who ElevenLabs is not the best fit for

If you only need occasional, simple narration and want a studio-style editor with stock music and slides, Murf AI may feel more straightforward. If your goal is listening to documents rather than publishing audio, Speechify is built for that. FAQ

Can I monetize YouTube videos made with the ElevenLabs free plan?

No. ElevenLabs says the free plan cannot be used for any commercial purpose, and a monetized channel is commercial use. Generate the audio on a paid plan instead.

Do I need to credit ElevenLabs on a paid plan?

ElevenLabs' help center requires attribution for free-plan content. On paid plans, attribution depends on your agreement with ElevenLabs; standard paid plans include a commercial license.

If I cancel my ElevenLabs subscription, can I keep using the audio?

According to ElevenLabs, content generated during a paid subscription can be used commercially indefinitely, subject to its Service-Specific Terms. Audio made before or after the subscription is not covered.

Can I clone someone else's voice for commercial use?

Only if you have the rights and permission to use that voice. ElevenLabs makes commercial use conditional on holding the necessary rights and following its Prohibited Use Policy.
