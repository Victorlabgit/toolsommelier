Fireflies.ai vs Otter.ai: which AI note taker fits how you meet?

Fireflies.ai vs Otter.ai compared on languages, live transcription, CRM integrations, free plans and who each one suits, so you pick the right note taker.

Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai are two of the most searched AI meeting assistants. Both join your Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, transcribe them and send you a summary. On paper they look alike. In practice, they are built around different habits.

Otter grew up as a live transcription app: you watch the transcript appear as people talk, on your laptop or your phone. Fireflies grew up as a meeting memory for teams: every call becomes searchable, and the notes flow into the tools your team already uses. That difference should drive your choice more than any feature list.

The short answer

Choose Fireflies.ai if your meetings happen in several languages, or if notes need to land in a CRM, Slack or a project tool, with a searchable library of every call.

Choose Otter.ai if you want to follow a transcript live, record in-person conversations from your phone, and you mostly meet in English or one of the few languages Otter supports.

Which one for your job?

  • Sales calls that must end up in HubSpot or Salesforce: both can do it on paid plans; Fireflies goes further with conversation analytics.
  • International team, calls in Portuguese, Italian or Hindi: Fireflies, because Otter's language list is short.
  • Students, journalists, researchers: Otter, for the live transcript and the phone app.
  • A solo user who just wants free notes: try both, and also look at Fathom in our full note taker guide.

How to choose

Ask one question: do you need the transcript during the meeting, or a memory of all your meetings afterwards? During → Otter. Afterwards, across a team and several languages → Fireflies. Both have free plans, so record the same call with each before you commit, and always tell participants they are being recorded.

FAQ

Is Fireflies better than Otter?

Neither is better overall. Fireflies suits teams that need many languages, integrations and a searchable call library; Otter suits people who want a live transcript and mobile recording.

Does Otter support French or Spanish?

Yes. Otter's pricing page lists English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Chinese for AI transcription. Fireflies covers many more languages.

Are Fireflies and Otter free?

Both have free plans with limits: Fireflies caps storage, Otter caps monthly minutes and the length of each conversation.

Can both join Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes. Both can join meetings on the three main platforms automatically and send notes afterwards.
