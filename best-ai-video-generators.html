"AI video" covers very different jobs. A training video with a talking presenter, a faceless YouTube Short made from a blog post, and a cinematic generated clip need completely different tools.

Here's how the main options pair with each type of video.

The short answer

  • Corporate training and presenter videos: Synthesia.
  • Personal avatars and video translation with lip-sync: HeyGen.
  • Turning articles and scripts into videos with stock footage: Pictory.
  • Creative, generated footage from text or images: Runway.
  • Editing videos you filmed yourself: Descript.

Sy

Best for business

Synthesia

Best for training, onboarding and internal communication videos. Free option: yes, limited free plan.

Structured and polished, a grand cru for the boardroom.

Synthesia turns a script into a video presented by a realistic AI avatar, in many languages. It's built for companies: templates, brand kits, collaboration and security features. If you need to produce and update dozens of training videos without a camera crew, it's the reference.

Why we like it
  • Professional avatars and templates
  • Many languages for global teams
  • Easy to update a video by editing the script
Watch out for
  • Avatar videos are not ideal for entertainment content
  • Advanced features sit in higher tiers
Visit Synthesia
Hg

Best for creators

HeyGen

Best for personal avatars, social videos and video translation. Free option: yes, limited free plan.

Lively and expressive, with an international bouquet.

HeyGen lets you create a digital twin of yourself and generate videos from text. Its video translation feature re-voices an existing video in another language with lip-sync, which is powerful for creators going international. It's more creator- and marketing-oriented than Synthesia.

Why we like it
  • Custom avatar of yourself
  • Video translation with lip-sync
  • Fast for social media formats
Watch out for
  • Credit limits on lower plans
  • Avatars still feel artificial in some long-form content
Visit HeyGen
Pi

Best for faceless content

Pictory

Best for turning blog posts and scripts into videos. Free option: free trial.

Practical and efficient, a reliable table wine.

Pictory takes a script, a blog post or a long video and turns it into short videos with stock footage, captions and an AI voiceover. It's a favorite for faceless YouTube channels and for repurposing written content into social clips.

Why we like it
  • Article-to-video in a few clicks
  • Automatic captions
  • Great for repurposing content
Watch out for
  • Stock footage can feel generic
  • Less creative control than a real editor
Visit Pictory
Ru

Best for creative work

Runway

Best for generated footage, visual effects and experimental clips. Free option: yes, limited credits.

Bold and avant-garde, for adventurous palates.

Runway is a creative suite for generating video from text or images and for AI-powered editing effects. It's the choice of filmmakers, designers and artists rather than of teams making training content.

Why we like it
  • High-quality generative video
  • Many AI editing tools in one place
  • Strong for ads and creative projects
Watch out for
  • Credits go quickly on generation
  • Generated clips are short and need assembling
Visit Runway
De

Best for editing

Descript

Best for editing videos you recorded yourself. Free option: yes.

The versatile house pour.

If you film yourself, Descript is often more useful than any generator: edit the video by editing its transcript, remove filler words, add captions and clean up audio automatically.

Why we like it
  • Edit video like a document
  • Captions and clean-up built in
  • Good free plan to start
Watch out for
  • Doesn't generate avatars or footage from scratch
  • Full editor has a learning curve
Visit Descript

How to choose

  • Do you want a person on screen without filming? Synthesia for business, HeyGen for personal branding.
  • Faceless channel or content repurposing? Pictory.
  • Creative or ad footage? Runway.
  • Already filming? Descript.

FAQ

What is the best AI video generator for beginners?

Pictory and HeyGen are among the easiest to start with: paste a script, pick a template, and you get a video in minutes.

Synthesia or HeyGen?

Synthesia is stronger for companies producing training and internal videos at scale; HeyGen is stronger for creators, personal avatars and video translation. See our full comparison.

Can AI video tools translate my videos?

Yes. HeyGen offers video translation with lip-sync, and ElevenLabs offers audio dubbing that keeps the speaker's voice.
