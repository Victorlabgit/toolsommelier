The best AI meeting note takers, paired to how you meet

An AI note taker joins your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams calls, records them, and gives you a transcript, a summary and action items. You stop taking notes and start listening.

All four tools below do the basics well. The difference is in who they're built for.

The short answer

  • Best free option: Fathom.
  • Best for sales and CRM workflows: Fireflies.ai.
  • Best for live transcription (classes, interviews): Otter.ai.
  • Best for multilingual and European teams: tl;dv.

Our picks, in detail

#1 · Best free option

Fathom

Best for: Individuals and small teams who want great notes at no cost

Tasting note: Crisp and refreshing, remarkable value for the price.

Fathom records and summarizes your calls on Zoom, Google Meet and Teams, and highlights key moments and action items. Its free plan is known for being unusually generous, making it the easiest place to start.

Why we like it
  • Generous free plan
  • Clean, fast summaries
  • Works with the main video platforms
Watch out for
  • Team and CRM features require paid plans
  • Fewer analytics than sales-focused tools
Try Fathom →
#2 · Best for sales

Fireflies.ai

Best for: Sales and customer teams that live in their CRM

Tasting note: Rich and well-connected, pairs with every CRM.

Fireflies transcribes, summarizes and makes all your calls searchable, then pushes notes into tools like CRMs and project managers. Conversation analytics help sales teams spot what works across calls.

Why we like it
  • Many integrations (CRM, Slack, project tools)
  • Searchable library of all calls
  • Conversation analytics
Watch out for
  • Free plan storage is limited
  • Can be more than a solo user needs
Try Fireflies.ai →
#3 · Best for live transcription

Otter.ai

Best for: Lectures, interviews and live captions

Tasting note: A classic, dependable vintage.

Otter is one of the original AI transcription tools. It transcribes in real time, so you can follow and highlight during the conversation, and it can join your meetings automatically to produce summaries.

Why we like it
  • Real-time transcript you can follow live
  • Mobile app for in-person recording
  • Automatic meeting summaries
Watch out for
  • Free minutes are limited
  • Language support is narrower than some rivals
Try Otter.ai →
#4 · Best for multilingual teams

tl;dv

Best for: Teams meeting in several languages

Tasting note: Cosmopolitan, with a European accent.

tl;dv records Google Meet, Zoom and Teams calls, generates AI notes and lets you clip and share key moments. It's a solid pick for teams that hold meetings in more than one language.

Why we like it
  • Multilingual transcription
  • Easy clips of key moments
  • Free plan to start
Watch out for
  • Advanced AI reports on paid plans
  • Integrations vary by plan
Try tl;dv →

How to choose

  • Just want great notes for free? Fathom.
  • Your notes need to land in a CRM? Fireflies.ai.
  • In-person recording or live captions? Otter.ai.
  • Meetings in several languages? tl;dv.
  • Always tell participants the meeting is being recorded: in many countries it's a legal requirement.

FAQ

What is the best free AI note taker?

Fathom is widely considered the most generous free AI meeting note taker; Fireflies, Otter and tl;dv also offer free plans with limits.

Do AI note takers work with Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes, all four tools in this guide support the major video meeting platforms.

Is it legal to record meetings with AI?

Rules depend on the country, but you should always inform participants and get consent before recording.
