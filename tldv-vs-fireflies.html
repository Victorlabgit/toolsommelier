tl;dv vs Fireflies.ai: which AI meeting recorder should you pick?

tl;dv vs Fireflies.ai compared on free plans, video recording, languages, CRM sync and clips, to help you choose the right AI meeting recorder for your team.

tl;dv and Fireflies.ai both record your Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, transcribe them and write AI notes. Both have a free plan, both sync to CRMs. So which one should you install?

The short version: tl;dv is built around video and sharing key moments, with a free plan that its makers describe as unlimited for recordings. Fireflies is built around searchable transcripts and integrations, with the widest language coverage of the two.

The short answer

Choose tl;dv if you want video recordings of your calls, clips of key moments to share with your team, and a generous free plan to start.

Choose Fireflies.ai if transcripts matter more than video, you meet in many languages, and you want notes pushed into lots of tools automatically.

FAQ

Is tl;dv really free?

Yes. tl;dv has a free plan that it describes as including unlimited recordings and transcriptions; paid plans add advanced AI reports and integrations.

Which supports more languages, tl;dv or Fireflies?

Fireflies cites 100+ transcription languages, while tl;dv cites 30+.

Yes. tl;dv offers a desktop app that records meetings without a bot appearing as a participant.
