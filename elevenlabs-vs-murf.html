ElevenLabs vs Murf AI: which AI voice generator should you pick?

ElevenLabs vs Murf AI compared on voice realism, languages, voice cloning, commercial rights and workflow, so you pick the right AI voice generator.

ElevenLabs and Murf AI are both serious AI voice generators, used for YouTube voiceovers, explainer videos, courses and ads. But they approach the job from opposite ends.

ElevenLabs is a voice lab: it focuses on the most natural, expressive speech possible, plus voice cloning and dubbing. Murf is a voiceover studio: it focuses on the workflow of producing a narration, syncing it to slides or video, and working on it as a team.

The short answer

Choose ElevenLabs if realism and emotion come first, if you want to clone your own voice, or if you publish in many languages and want dubbing.

Choose Murf AI if you produce training, e-learning or explainer narrations as a team and want a timeline to sync voice with slides and video.

Which one for your project?

  • YouTube narration, audiobooks, character voices: ElevenLabs. See also whether you can use ElevenLabs commercially.
  • Corporate training and onboarding modules: Murf, for its studio and team features.
  • Translating your videos into other languages with your own voice: ElevenLabs dubbing.
  • Slide decks with a voiceover: Murf.

How to choose

Paste the same 60-second script into both free plans and listen. If the difference in realism matters for your audience, pick ElevenLabs. If you care more about producing narrations quickly and editing them with colleagues, pick Murf.

FAQ

Is ElevenLabs better than Murf?

For pure voice realism and cloning, ElevenLabs is usually ahead. For producing synced voiceovers as a team, Murf's studio is more convenient.

Can I use ElevenLabs or Murf voices commercially?

Yes, on paid plans. ElevenLabs' free plan does not include a commercial licence, and Murf includes commercial rights on its paid plans.

Which has more languages?

ElevenLabs lists more than 70 languages on its pricing page; Murf lists 35+ on its website.

Can I clone my voice on Murf?

Yes, but Murf's voice cloning is reserved for higher-tier plans. ElevenLabs offers instant voice cloning from its first paid plan.
