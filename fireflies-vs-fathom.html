Fireflies.ai vs Fathom: which AI note taker should you pick?

Fireflies.ai vs Fathom compared on free plans, languages, video, CRM sync and team features, so you can choose the right AI meeting note taker for your calls.

Fireflies.ai and Fathom are two of the AI note takers people compare most often. Both join your Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, record them, and send you a summary when the meeting ends. Both have a free plan. So where is the real difference?

Fathom is built around a generous free plan for individuals: per Fathom, recordings, transcription and storage are unlimited, with clean summaries and an option to record without a bot. Fireflies is built around breadth: 100+ transcription languages (per Fireflies), file uploads, a large integration library and analytics for sales and customer teams on its higher plans.

The short answer

Choose Fireflies.ai if your meetings run in many languages, if you also want to transcribe uploaded audio or video files, or if your team needs notes pushed into lots of tools and conversation analytics across calls.

Choose Fathom if you mostly meet in English or another major European language and want the most generous free plan for yourself, with unlimited recordings and the option to record without a bot in the call.

Which one for your job?

  • Freelancer or consultant who wants free notes on every call: Fathom.
  • International team with calls in Hindi, Japanese or Arabic: Fireflies, because Fathom's native language list is short.
  • Podcasts, interviews or recordings you already have: Fireflies, which accepts file uploads.
  • Sales team that wants CRM updates and coaching: both put this on their Business plan, so trial both on real deals.

How to choose

Start with two questions: which languages do you meet in, and how much do you want to pay? English or a major European language, and you want the most for free → Fathom. Many languages, uploaded files, or a team that lives in its integrations → Fireflies. Both have free plans, so record the same call with each, and always tell participants they are being recorded.

FAQ

Is Fathom really free?

Yes. Fathom's help center says its free version includes unlimited storage and recordings, and its pricing page lists unlimited transcription and AI summaries. Team features and CRM sync require a paid plan.

Which supports more languages, Fireflies or Fathom?

Fireflies, by a wide margin: it cites automatic detection of 100+ languages, while Fathom lists seven native transcription languages.

Can Fathom or Fireflies record without a bot joining the call?

Both offer an option. Fathom records without a bot through its Mac desktop app, and Fireflies through its Chrome extension.

Do Fireflies and Fathom sync with HubSpot and Salesforce?

Yes. Both reserve automatic CRM updates for HubSpot and Salesforce to their Business plans and above.
