Synthesia vs HeyGen: which AI avatar tool should you pick?

Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

Synthesia and HeyGen are the two best-known AI avatar video platforms. Both turn a script into a video with a realistic presenter. But they are aimed at different people, and that's what should decide your choice.

The short answer

Choose Synthesia if you're a company producing training, onboarding or internal videos at scale, with brand consistency and collaboration in mind.

Choose HeyGen if you're a creator or marketer who wants your own avatar, social-ready formats and video translation with lip-sync.

Our picks, in detail

#1 · Pick for companies

Synthesia

Best for: L&D teams, HR, internal comms

Tasting note: Elegant and structured. Built to age well in a corporate cellar.

Synthesia's strengths are on the business side: professional templates, brand kits, team workspaces, and an emphasis on security and compliance that large organizations expect. Updating a video is as simple as editing the script, which matters when your training content changes every quarter.

Why we like it
  • Business-grade templates and brand kits
  • Collaboration for teams
  • Easy updates by editing text
Watch out for
  • Less suited to personal-brand social content
  • Best features on higher plans

#2 · Pick for creators

HeyGen

Best for: Creators, marketers, personal branding

Tasting note: Vibrant and expressive, made to be shared.

HeyGen shines when the video is about you: create a digital twin, produce short social clips quickly, and translate a video into other languages with your own voice and lip movements. It's the more flexible, creator-friendly option.

Why we like it
  • Personal avatar from a short recording
  • Video translation with lip-sync
  • Fast social formats
Watch out for

  • Fewer enterprise-style governance features

How to choose

Ask yourself one question: is the video about your company's knowledge, or about you? Company knowledge → Synthesia. Personal brand and audience → HeyGen. Both have free options, so make the same 30-second video in each before committing.

FAQ

Is HeyGen better than Synthesia?

Not overall. Each is better for a different audience: HeyGen for creators and translation, Synthesia for companies producing training content.

Can I try both for free?

Yes, both offer a limited free option, enough to compare avatar quality on your own script.
