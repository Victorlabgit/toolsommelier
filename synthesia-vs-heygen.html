Synthesia and HeyGen are the two best-known AI avatar video platforms. Both turn a script into a video with a realistic presenter. But they are aimed at different people, and that's what should decide your choice.

The short answer

Choose Synthesia if you're a company producing training, onboarding or internal videos at scale, with brand consistency and collaboration in mind.

Choose HeyGen if you're a creator or marketer who wants your own avatar, social-ready formats and video translation with lip-sync.

How to choose

Ask yourself one question: is the video about your company's knowledge, or about you? Company knowledge → Synthesia. Personal brand and audience → HeyGen. Both have free options, so make the same 30-second video in each before committing.

FAQ

Is HeyGen better than Synthesia?

Not overall. Each is better for a different audience: HeyGen for creators and translation, Synthesia for companies producing training content.

Can I try both for free?

Yes, both offer a limited free option, enough to compare avatar quality on your own script.
