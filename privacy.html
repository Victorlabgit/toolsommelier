Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not collect payment information. The only personal data we collect is your email address, and only if you choose to subscribe to our newsletter.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to The Weekly Pairing, your email address is collected and stored by our newsletter provider, beehiiv (Beehiiv Inc., United States), which sends the emails on our behalf. We use it only to send you the newsletter. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)): you confirm your subscription by clicking a link in a confirmation email (double opt-in), and you can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email, after which your address is no longer used for sending. beehiiv may record whether emails are opened and links clicked, to help us understand what readers find useful. See beehiiv's privacy policy. The signup form on our pages is loaded from beehiiv's servers.

Audience measurement

We count visits and clicks on outbound links with Umami Cloud (Umami Software, data hosted in the EU), a privacy-friendly analytics service that uses no cookies, does not track you across websites and does not collect personal data such as your IP address in a way that identifies you. We use these aggregated statistics only to understand which pages and recommendations are useful. See Umami's privacy policy.

Fonts

Our typefaces are served by Bunny Fonts (BunnyWay d.o.o., EU), a privacy-focused font service that does not use cookies or track visitors.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. When you click an outbound link to a software vendor, that vendor's own website may set cookies (including affiliate tracking cookies) under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you. For newsletter subscribers, you can unsubscribe at any time or write to contact@toolsommelier.com to have your address deleted. See our legal notice for publisher and hosting details.
