How we pick AI tools

The Tool Sommelier helps you choose AI software the way a good sommelier chooses a wine: by starting from what you need, not from what is loudest.

Our method

  • Job first. Every guide answers one concrete need: creating voiceovers, recording meetings, producing videos, writing content.
  • Primary sources. We read each tool's official documentation, feature pages and pricing pages, and cross-check them with independent user reviews.
  • Trade-offs, not hype. Every pick lists what it does well and where it falls short.
  • Kept fresh. AI tools change monthly. Each guide shows the date of its last update, and we revise guides as plans and features change.

How we make money

Some links are affiliate links. If you subscribe through them, the vendor may pay us a commission. You pay the same price. Commissions never decide a ranking: a tool with no affiliate program can, and does, rank first. See our affiliate disclosure.

Spotted an error?

Prices and features move fast. We review every guide regularly and fix anything that has changed.
